1. SERVICES
================================================================================

1.1 Scope of Services
Provider agrees to provide the following services to Client:

(a) Healthcare Data Platform
- Cloud-hosted data warehouse for clinical and operational data
- Real-time analytics and reporting dashboards
- HIPAA-compliant data storage and processing

(b) Integration Services
- EHR system integration (Epic, Cerner, Meditech)
- Medical device data connectivity
- Third-party application APIs

(c) Professional Services
- Implementation and configuration
- Data migration and validation
- Training and change management

1.2 Service Levels
Provider commits to the following service levels:
- Platform Availability: 99.9% uptime
- Support Response: Critical issues within 1 hour
- Data Recovery: RPO < 1 hour, RTO < 4 hours

2. TERM AND TERMINATION
================================================================================

2.1 Initial Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of three (3) years.

2.2 Renewal
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.

2.4 Termination for Convenience
Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon one hundred eighty (180) days prior written notice, subject to payment of early termination fees as specified in Schedule B.

================================================================================
3. FEES AND PAYMENT
================================================================================

3.1 Fees
Client agrees to pay the fees set forth in Schedule A:

Platform Subscription:        $450,000 per year
Integration Services:         $125,000 (one-time)
Professional Services:        $275/hour
Annual Support & Maintenance: $85,000 per year

3.2 Payment Terms
- Subscription fees billed annually in advance
- Professional services billed monthly in arrears
- Payment due within thirty (30) days of invoice
- Late payments subject to 1.5% monthly interest

3.3 Price Adjustments
Provider may increase subscription fees by up to 5% annually upon sixty (60) days written notice prior to renewal.

4. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY
================================================================================

4.1 HIPAA Compliance
Provider shall maintain compliance with HIPAA Privacy Rule, Security Rule, and Breach Notification Rule. A Business Associate Agreement is attached as Schedule C.

4.2 Data Ownership
All Client data remains the property of Client. Provider acquires no rights to Client data except as necessary to perform the Services.

4.3 Security Measures
Provider shall implement and maintain:
- SOC 2 Type II certified data centers
- End-to-end encryption (TLS 1.3, AES-256)
- Multi-factor authentication
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments

4.4 Data Location
All Client data shall be stored and processed within the United States.

4.5 Breach Notification
Provider shall notify Client of any security breach affecting Client data within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.
