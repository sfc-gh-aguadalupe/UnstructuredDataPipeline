NEXGEN TECHNOLOGIES, INC.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NexGen Technologies delivered strong fourth quarter results, exceeding analyst expectations across key financial metrics. Revenue growth accelerated driven by robust demand for our cloud platform and enterprise AI solutions.

Key Highlights:
- Revenue: $847.3 million (up 34% year-over-year)
- Gross Margin: 72.4% (up 180 basis points)
- Operating Income: $156.2 million (up 52% year-over-year)
- Net Income: $124.8 million ($2.15 per diluted share)
- Free Cash Flow: $198.4 million

"Q4 was an exceptional quarter that demonstrates the strength of our platform strategy," said CEO Amanda Foster. "Our investments in AI capabilities are paying off, with enterprise customers increasingly adopting our full suite of solutions."
