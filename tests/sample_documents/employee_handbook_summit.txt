SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
================================================================================

1.1 Equal Employment Opportunity

Summit Consulting Group is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other protected characteristic.

1.2 At-Will Employment

Employment with Summit is at-will, meaning either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.

1.3 Background Checks

All offers of employment are contingent upon successful completion of:
- Criminal background check
- Employment verification
- Education verification
- Reference checks
- Drug screening (where applicable)

================================================================================
SECTION 2: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
================================================================================

2.1 Pay Periods

Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. Direct deposit is required for all employees.

2.2 Health Insurance

Summit offers comprehensive health benefits effective the first of the month following 30 days of employment:

Medical Plans:
- PPO Plan: $250/month employee, $650/month family
- HDHP with HSA: $150/month employee, $450/month family
- Company contributes $1,500/year to HSA

Dental and Vision:
- Dental PPO: $25/month employee
- Vision: $10/month employee

2.3 Retirement Benefits

401(k) Plan:
- Eligible after 90 days of employment
- Company matches 100% of first 4% of contributions
- Immediate vesting of company match

2.4 Paid Time Off

PTO Accrual (based on years of service):
- 0-2 years: 15 days per year
- 3-5 years: 20 days per year
- 6+ years: 25 days per year

Additional Leave:
- 10 paid holidays per year
- 5 sick days per year
- Bereavement leave: 3-5 days
- Jury duty: As needed with full pay

2.5 Professional Development

Summit invests in employee growth:
- Annual training budget: $3,000 per employee
- Certification reimbursement: Up to $5,000
- Tuition assistance: Up to $10,000/year for approved programs
- Conference attendance support

================================================================================
SECTION 3: WORKPLACE CONDUCT
================================================================================

3.1 Professional Standards

Employees are expected to:
- Maintain professional appearance and demeanor
- Treat colleagues and clients with respect
- Protect confidential information
- Represent Summit positively in all interactions

3.2 Attendance and Punctuality

Regular attendance is essential. Employees must:
- Notify supervisor of absences before shift start
- Provide documentation for extended absences
- Request planned time off at least 2 weeks in advance

3.3 Remote Work Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week:
- Manager approval required
- Must maintain core hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Required in-office days: Tuesday and Thursday
- Home workspace must meet security requirements

3.4 Anti-Harassment Policy

Summit maintains zero tolerance for harassment of any kind. Harassment includes:
- Unwelcome verbal, physical, or visual conduct
- Conduct that creates a hostile work environment
- Quid pro quo harassment
- Retaliation against those who report harassment

Report incidents to HR immediately. All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: TECHNOLOGY AND SECURITY
================================================================================

4.1 Acceptable Use Policy

Company technology resources are for business purposes. Employees must:
- Protect passwords and access credentials
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately
- Not install unauthorized software
- Not access inappropriate content

4.2 Confidentiality

Employees have access to confidential information including:
- Client data and strategies
- Financial information
- Employee records
- Trade secrets

This information must be protected during and after employment.

4.3 Social Media

When using social media:
- Do not disclose confidential information
- Clearly state opinions are your own
- Do not speak on behalf of Summit without authorization
- Maintain professional conduct

================================================================================
SECTION 5: HEALTH AND SAFETY
================================================================================

5.1 Workplace Safety

Summit is committed to providing a safe workplace. Employees must:
- Report unsafe conditions immediately
- Follow safety procedures
- Complete required safety training
- Participate in emergency drills

5.2 Drug-Free Workplace

Summit maintains a drug-free workplace. The use, possession, or distribution of illegal drugs or alcohol on company premises is prohibited and grounds for immediate termination.

5.3 Workplace Violence Prevention

Threats, intimidation, or violence are strictly prohibited. Report concerns to HR or Security immediately.
